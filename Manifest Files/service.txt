A Service gives a stable network endpoint for accessing matching Pods.

apiVersion: v1
kind: Service
metadata:
  name: nginx-service
spec:
  type: NodePort
  selector:
    app: nginx-deploy
  ports:
    - port: 80
      targetPort: 80
      nodePort: 30080



Apply this after creating the Deployment in Example 3:
kubectl apply -f service.yml
kubectl get services
kubectl describe service nginx-service


Access it with Minikube:
minikube service nginx-service


The Service selector app: nginx-deploy matches the Deployment's Pod labels.
- port: Service port.
- targetPort: destination port on the Pod.
- nodePort: port exposed on the node, in the permitted NodePort range.
